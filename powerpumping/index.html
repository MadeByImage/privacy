Privacy Policy

Power Pumping  ·  Effective date: 20 May 2026
Short version: Power Pumping stores all data locally on your device. No personal information is collected, transmitted, or shared with anyone.

Overview

Power Pumping is a free breastfeeding timer app built by Made By Image. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Power Pumping does not collect any personal information.

All data you enter — session history, milk amounts, custom schedules, and reminder times — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to Made By Image or any third party.

  • The app does not require an account or login
  • The app does not transmit any data over the internet
  • The app contains no advertising
  • The app uses no analytics or tracking services

Third Party Services

Power Pumping does not use any third-party services that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Power Pumping is intended for use by adults. The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. When we make changes we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

If we add features in future updates that involve data collection — such as cloud sync or analytics — this policy will be updated before those features are released.
