Privacy Policy

Zappr  ·  Effective date: 8 June 2026
Short version: Zappr communicates only with your TV on your local network. No personal information is collected, transmitted to external servers, or shared with anyone.

Overview

Zappr is an Android TV remote control app built by Made By Image. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

Zappr does not collect any personal information.

The only data the app stores is pairing information for your TV — such as a local device name and the credentials required to authenticate with it. This data is stored exclusively on your device using secure local storage and never leaves your device.

  • The app does not require an account or login
  • The app communicates only with devices on your local network
  • No data is transmitted to Made By Image or any external server
  • The app contains no advertising
  • The app uses no analytics or tracking services

Local Network Access

Zappr requires access to your local network in order to discover and connect to Android TV devices. This access is used solely to communicate with your TV. The app does not scan, monitor, or transmit information about other devices on your network.

Third Party Services

Zappr does not use any third-party services that collect data.

Children's Privacy

Zappr does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. When we make changes we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

If we add features in future updates that involve data collection — such as cloud sync or analytics — this policy will be updated before those features are released.
